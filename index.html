    How we met

    Gabriella was born and raised in Houston, Texas, and Brandon is a St. Louis native — two cities, two roots, one love story. We met when Gabby visited St. Louis, and from the very first night it was clear this was something special. True love has a funny way of showing up when you least expect it, and what started as one unforgettable visit turned into forever.

    I am so incredibly fortunate that Brandon had the courage and independence to pick up his life and move to Texas on his own. Watching him build a new career, plant new roots, and continuously grow into the best version of himself has been one of the greatest honors of my life. He didn't just move to a new state — he chose us, and I will never stop being grateful for that.
